# 21 Sticks Nim

## Project summary

A simple console-mode implementation, in Kotlin, of a Nim variant called "21 Sticks".

## Rules

In 21 Sticks, play starts with a single pile of 21 sticks. The two players take turns removing 1, 2, or 3 sticks from the pile. The player removing the last stick from the pile loses the game.
